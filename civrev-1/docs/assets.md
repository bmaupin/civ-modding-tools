# Assets

## General

- .fpk files are version 6 and can be extracted with dragon unpacker ([see here](https://github.com/bmaupin/civ-modding-tools/blob/main/civilization-5/assets.md))
- .fpk files seem to contain most assets
  - .dds, .xml, .mp3, .wav
  - Models: .gr2, .nxb
- Shader files (.fxobj)
- A few mp3s

## FPKs
